After trialling an apple-harvesting robot in Hawke's Bay, New Zealand, we had more apples than we needed. A few other engineers and I decided to make cider and give it to departing members of the team.

I was in charge of packaging and marketing. After conducting zero market research and consulting a focus group having only myself as a member, the brand Garden7 was decided. The logo uses a robot arm for the seven and appears on the bottle labels. For the gift boxes, I replaced the wooden lids with acrylic windows, then engraved the logo and a message using my CNC cutter.
